How to fight
  • Each round you draw one attack and up to two guards on the pad. Your foe secretly does the same.
  • An attack on an unguarded line hits. An attack on a guarded line is parried and countered.
  • Your first guard is your main guard. Parry with it and you riposte for 1.5× counter damage.
  • Everything costs stamina: attacks 1 to 3, each guard 1. You regain 3 a round. Tap Rest to skip your attack and regain 2 more.
  • Read the foe's stance: sword raised high means chop, held back level means slash, held low means rising cut, high and angled means falling cut, pulled back means thrust. Some foes lie.
  • A fast, straight stroke is clean and deals 25% more damage. Lighter attacks strike first.
